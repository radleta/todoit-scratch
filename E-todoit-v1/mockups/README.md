# todoit look mockups

Four static directions for the `visual-aesthetic-mockups` spike. Each shows the same content: the add input, five active todos as bubbles, three done todos with a restore button, all/active/done filters with counts (8, 5, 3), the all-time popped tally (128), a mute toggle, and an empty-state inset. Open any file from disk; none loads anything from the network.

- **tidepool.html**: underwater. Air bubbles rise through teal-to-abyss water under faint light shafts; deep blue `#06283D`, lagoon `#1B8A9A`, foam `#E6FBFF`, kelp and coral accents. Calm and dreamy, with done todos "surfaced".
- **washday.html**: soap bubbles on a sunny washday. Iridescent pink, mint and lilac rims on a pale sky with a sun and green hills, navy ink `#1E3A5F` text. The lightest and most playful of the four.
- **orbit.html**: space. Todos are small planets (one ringed) on a starfield over violet `#140C33`, with sun-amber `#FFB347` accents and geometric type. Done todos turn to "stardust".
- **plain.html**: abstract, for anyone who would rather skip a theme. Flat Bauhaus-style circles in blue, yellow, red, green and graphite on cool grey paper `#EEF0F3`, with a Swiss sidebar layout and plain all/active/done wording.
